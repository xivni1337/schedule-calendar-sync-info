# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Schedule Calendar Sync is a personal automation used to add and update university schedule events in the account owner's Google Calendar.

## Data accessed

The app requests access to Google Calendar only so it can create, update, and remove schedule events that it manages. It also reads a schedule source chosen by the account owner.

## Data storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted repository secrets and are used only for automated calendar synchronization. The app does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

## Retention and deletion

The account owner controls the source data, calendar events, and OAuth access. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account connections page. The automation and its stored credentials can also be deleted by the account owner.

## Contact

Questions about this app can be submitted through the owning GitHub account.
